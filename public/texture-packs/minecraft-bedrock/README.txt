Minecraft Bedrock texture pack for NodeZtudio

Source: https://github.com/Mojang/bedrock-samples/tree/46ba6ea985fb5a92d79a9419198f10dda14c199d/resource_pack/textures/blocks
Revision: 46ba6ea985fb5a92d79a9419198f10dda14c199d
1334 block definitions, 321 texture variants, 1298 color textures.
The editor uses cube geometry, static first animation frames, and fixed foliage/water colors.
Invisible blocks and unresolved upstream definitions are listed in pack.json.
Original Mojang assets are retained under original/. Normalized browser PNGs are under textures/.
